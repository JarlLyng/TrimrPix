# TrimrPix Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

This is the privacy policy for **TrimrPix for Mac**. A formatted version is at
<https://trimrpix.iamjarl.com/privacy.html>, and the two say the same thing.

Looking for the iPhone or iPad app? It is a separate app with its own policy:
<https://trimrpixforios.iamjarl.com/privacy.html>

## Overview

TrimrPix is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how
TrimrPix handles your data.

## Data collection

**Your images never leave your Mac.** TrimrPix compresses them on your Mac and
uploads nothing of them: no image, no file name, no folder and no metadata.

When your Mac is online, the app sends a little anonymous data to Sentry
(https://sentry.io/privacy/), a service for crash reports, so I learn when a
release breaks and which features earn their place:

- **Crash reports and diagnostics:** what went wrong in the app, Apple's
  diagnostics for hangs, CPU and disk use, and whether a session ended in a
  crash, with the app version, the macOS version, the Mac model and its locale.
- **Two counts:** that an image was optimized, with its output format (for
  example JPEG), and how many kilobytes it saved. Never what the image was.

None of it identifies you or your Mac. Every install reports as the same user,
"anonymous", with no installation ID, device ID or advertising ID, and no IP
address is stored. It isn't linked to you, it isn't used for tracking, and
nothing is kept on your Mac just to measure. There is no account. On the App
Store this is labelled Data Not Linked to You: Diagnostics and Usage Data.

This website is separate from the app. It uses Umami (https://umami.is/), a
privacy-focused analytics tool, to count page views and see which pages people
find useful. It does not use cookies for this, and it does not build a profile
of you.

## File access

TrimrPix only accesses files that you explicitly drag and drop, select via the
file picker, or add to a watched folder. All files are processed locally and
never leave your Mac.

## Data storage

User settings (for example compression preferences and the watch folder) are
stored locally on your device. No images or file content are stored
permanently, and nothing about your images is sent anywhere.
