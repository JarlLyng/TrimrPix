# TrimrPix Privacy Policy

_Last updated: February 2026_

This is the privacy policy for **TrimrPix for Mac**. A formatted version is at
<https://trimrpix.iamjarl.com/privacy.html>, and the two say the same thing.

Looking for the iPhone or iPad app? It is a separate app with its own policy:
<https://trimrpixforios.iamjarl.com/privacy.html>

## Overview

TrimrPix is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how
TrimrPix handles your data.

## Data collection

**The TrimrPix app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

- No data is collected
- No analytics or tracking in the app
- The app makes no network requests
- All processing is performed locally on your device

This website is separate from the app. It uses Umami (https://umami.is/), a
privacy-focused analytics tool, to count page views and see which pages people
find useful. It does not use cookies for this, and it does not build a profile
of you. The app itself talks to nothing.

## File access

TrimrPix only accesses files that you explicitly drag and drop, select via the
file picker, or add to a watched folder. All files are processed locally and
never leave your Mac.

## Data storage

User settings (for example compression preferences and the watch folder) are
stored locally on your device. No images or file content are stored
permanently. No data is sent to any server.
