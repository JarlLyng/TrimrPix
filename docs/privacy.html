Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Overview

TrimrPix is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how TrimrPix handles your data.

Data collection

The TrimrPix app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • No data is collected
  • No analytics or tracking in the app
  • The app makes no network requests
  • All processing is performed locally on your device

This website is separate from the app. It uses Umami, a privacy-focused analytics tool, to count page views and see which pages people find useful. It does not use cookies for this, and it does not build a profile of you. The app itself talks to nothing.

File access

TrimrPix only accesses files that you explicitly drag and drop, select via the file picker, or add to a watched folder. All files are processed locally and never leave your Mac.

Data storage

User settings (e.g. compression preferences, watch folder) are stored locally on your device. No images or file content are stored permanently. No data is sent to any server.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@iamjarl.com.
