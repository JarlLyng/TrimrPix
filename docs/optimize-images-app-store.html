How to Optimize Images for the App Store

Updated March 2026 · by IAMJARL

To optimize App Store screenshots, export them as PNG at the required resolution, then compress with TrimrPix to reduce file size without visible quality loss. This speeds up uploads and keeps your App Store Connect workflow fast.

App Store screenshot requirements

Device Size (pixels) Required format
iPhone 6.7″ 1290 × 2796 PNG or JPEG
iPhone 6.5″ 1242 × 2688 PNG or JPEG
iPad Pro 12.9″ 2048 × 2732 PNG or JPEG
Mac 1280 × 800 or 1440 × 900 PNG or JPEG

Why optimize App Store images?

App Store screenshots exported from design tools (Figma, Sketch, Xcode) are often much larger than necessary. Unoptimized PNGs at Retina resolution can easily be 5–10 MB each. This slows down App Store Connect uploads and adds unnecessary weight to your submission.

Compressing screenshots before uploading reduces file sizes significantly while keeping the visual quality Apple requires.

Step-by-step with TrimrPix

  1. Export screenshots from your design tool at the required resolution
  2. Drag all screenshots into TrimrPix
  3. Set quality to 90–95% for App Store (high quality needed)
  4. Click Optimize
  5. Upload the compressed screenshots to App Store Connect

Compression tips for developers

  • Keep quality high (90–95%) — App Store images are viewed at full resolution and scrutinized during review
  • PNG is recommended for screenshots — TrimrPix optimizes PNG losslessly, removing unnecessary metadata and reducing size
  • Use batch mode to optimize all device sizes at once — iPhone, iPad, and Mac screenshots in one pass
  • For marketing web images (not the App Store itself), consider WebP for even smaller files
